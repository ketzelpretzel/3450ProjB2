A brief educational introduction to anti-fascism, democratic freedoms, and the mission behind this fictional organization.

Anti-fascism is a broad tradition of opposition to fascist ideology and authoritarian politics. It includes many different movements and approaches, rather than a single centralized organization.

This educational website is a fictional design prototype. Anti-fascist activity has no single central leadership or universal membership.

1920s–1930s / Early anti-fascist movements

Opposition to emerging fascist parties took many forms across Europe, including labor organizing, political opposition, and community resistance.

1940s / Defending against fascist rule

Anti-fascist resistance was part of a wider historical struggle during World War II, alongside the efforts of Allied nations and resistance movements.

Today / Civic participation

Modern discussions of anti-fascism encompass civil liberties, democratic accountability, historical memory, and disagreement about political tactics.

This overview is introductory, not a complete historical account.

Recognize authoritarianism. Resist political oppression. Defend the freedoms of all people.
