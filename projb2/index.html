What is antifa?

Antifa is short for anti-fascist . It describes opposition to fascism—an authoritarian form of politics associated with extreme nationalism, suppression of opposition, and the rejection of democratic freedoms.

Antifa is not one single organization with a national leader or membership list. The term is used by different individuals and groups, whose beliefs and approaches can vary. Anti-fascist activity has included education, art, organizing, demonstrations, and other forms of political action.

Understanding the history behind the term makes it easier to recognize the symbols, movements, and debates associated with it today.
